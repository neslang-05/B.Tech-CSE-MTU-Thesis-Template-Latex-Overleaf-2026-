This appendix documents the database schema and data dictionary supporting the application's persistence layer. Tables are designed in Third Normal Form (3NF) to ensure integrity.

\section*{1. Users Table (\texttt{users})}
Stores core user details, credential hashes, and system access role designations.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Data Dictionary for \texttt{users} Table}
  \label{tab:db-users}
  \small
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.1}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|l|c|X|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Column Name} & \textbf{Data Type} & \textbf{Nullable} & \textbf{Description \& Constraints} \\
    \hline
    \texttt{id} & \texttt{UUID} & No & Unique identifier (Primary Key). \\
    \texttt{username} & \texttt{VARCHAR(50)} & No & Unique username for authentication. \\
    \texttt{email} & \texttt{VARCHAR(100)} & No & Unique email address. \\
    \texttt{password\_hash} & \texttt{VARCHAR(255)} & No & Secure Argon2 or bcrypt salted hash value. \\
    \texttt{role} & \texttt{VARCHAR(20)} & No & System authorization role (e.g., 'Admin', 'User'). \\
    \texttt{created\_at} & \texttt{TIMESTAMP} & No & Datetime of record creation. Defaults to current time. \\
    \hline
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section*{2. Transactions Log Table (\texttt{activity\_logs})}
Maintains a system audit trail to monitor requests and resource access details.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Data Dictionary for \texttt{activity\_logs} Table}
  \label{tab:db-logs}
  \small
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.1}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|l|c|X|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Column Name} & \textbf{Data Type} & \textbf{Nullable} & \textbf{Description \& Constraints} \\
    \hline
    \texttt{log\_id} & \texttt{BIGINT} & No & Auto-incrementing identifier (Primary Key). \\
    \texttt{user\_id} & \texttt{UUID} & Yes & Reference key (Foreign Key linking to \texttt{users.id}). \\
    \texttt{action} & \texttt{VARCHAR(100)} & No & Description of action executed (e.g., 'FILE\_UPLOAD'). \\
    \texttt{ip\_address} & \texttt{VARCHAR(45)} & No & Origin IP address of client requesting request. \\
    \texttt{timestamp} & \texttt{TIMESTAMP} & No & Datetime when log was written. \\
    \hline
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
