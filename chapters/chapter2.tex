\section{Research Gap}

\begin{itemize}

    \item There is a lack of localized datasets specific to the Northeastern region of India or Manipur.
    \item Existing applications lack offline capabilities or SMS-based feedback systems for rural users.
\end{itemize}
Your project aims to bridge these gaps by introducing an integrated, user-friendly, and localized system.
