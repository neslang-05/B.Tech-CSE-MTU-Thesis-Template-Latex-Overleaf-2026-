\noindent
An abstract is a self-contained, short summary of the entire thesis. It should typically be between 200 to 300 words and present a concise overview of the problem, the methodology, the major results, and the main conclusions.

A good abstract should answer the following questions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{What is the problem and why is it important?} (Context and Motivation)
    \item \textbf{What did you do and how did you do it?} (Methodology / System Design)
    \item \textbf{What did you find?} (Key Results and Evaluation Metrics)
    \item \textbf{What are the main implications or conclusions of your work?} (Significance)
\end{enumerate}
