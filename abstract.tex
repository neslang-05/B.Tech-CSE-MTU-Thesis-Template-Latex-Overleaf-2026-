Many small and marginal farmers often suffer crop losses due to substandard or counterfeit seeds that closely resemble certified products. Lacking affordable and rapid verification methods, farmers rely on packaging labels and basic visual inspection, which frequently fail to indicate true seed quality. As a result, poor-quality seeds are typically identified only after sowing, leading to low germination, reduced yield and financial loss. 

\textbf{AI-Based Paddy Seed Quality Assessment and Farmer Feedback System} is a comprehensive web platform designed to combat the widespread issue of counterfeit and substandard paddy seeds.This project aims to develop an AI-assisted seed quality assessment and farmer feedback system focused on early risk indication and post-use field feedback. Farmers can scan seed samples using a simple image-based interface to receive an estimated Quality Assessment of paddy seeds, presented in clear, farmer-friendly language. Additionally, a Farmer Complaint and Feedback Module enables reporting of field-level issues such as poor germination or crop failure which are combined into a dashboard to identify regional and batch-level risk patterns. By avoiding chemical analysis, specialized hardware or complex supply-chain systems, the proposed solution remains low-cost, scalable and suitable for rural deployment.
